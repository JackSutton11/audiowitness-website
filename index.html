AudioWitness

Silent personal safety recording for Android.

What it does

Triple-press the power button to silently start recording audio with the screen off. A second triple-press stops it. Recordings are saved with timestamp and location, automatically backed up to your Google Drive, and can be shared instantly with trusted contacts.

How recordings travel

When someone shares an AudioWitness recording with you, this site routes the link to the app if you have it installed. Otherwise the audio plays in your browser.

Privacy

Recordings stay on your device and your own Google Drive. We have no access to your data. Read the privacy policy.
