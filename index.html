AudioWitness

One gesture. Screen off. Silent. Recorded.

A discreet incident recorder for Android. No unlocking. No screen. No sound.

Your data stays yours. AudioWitness has no servers, no databases, no analytics, and no tracking. Recordings live only on your device and — if you choose — in your own Google Drive. We never see them.

What it does

Triple-press your power button and AudioWitness starts recording audio — without opening the app, lighting up your screen, or making a sound. Triple-press again to stop. A quiet vibration confirms each action. That is the whole interaction.

It is built for moments when you need an accurate record of what happened but can't safely hold a phone to make one. People reach for it in personal safety situations, disputes, confrontations, and harassment. AudioWitness does not call for help, alert anyone, or intervene — it is not an alarm or a protection service. What it does is make the record, discreetly, so you have it afterward.

How it works

  • Triple-press the power button to start recording — the screen stays off
  • Triple-press again to stop — a short vibration confirms each action
  • Recordings are timestamped and location-stamped automatically
  • Each recording is backed up to your own Google Drive (optional)
  • Recordings can be sent automatically to a trusted contact by SMS
  • Review recordings in the built-in player, tag key moments, and see where each was made on a map

Privacy

AudioWitness collects nothing about you. Everything stays on your device and, only if you choose, in your own Google Drive — where the app can see only the files it creates, nothing else. When a recording is shared, it travels directly from your device to your trusted contact. It never passes through us.

What AudioWitness is not

It is not a personal alarm, a panic button, or an emergency service — it does not contact anyone for help or intervene in any way. It is not a hidden spy tool or a secret call recorder. It records your own surroundings, on your own device, by your own deliberate action. Record responsibly and in line with the laws that apply where you are — recording consent laws vary by country and state.
